Privacy Policy — Gmail Attachment Puller

This application accesses Gmail data using the read-only gmail.readonly scope, solely for the account owner's own personal use.

  • Data accessed: email metadata and attachment contents, read directly from the owner's own Gmail account.
  • Data storage: attachments are saved locally to the owner's own computer. No data is transmitted to, stored on, or shared with any third party, server, or service.
  • Data use: solely to locate and download specific attachments the account owner requests.
  • No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
